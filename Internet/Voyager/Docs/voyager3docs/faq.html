8 Historical FAQ

This chapter is a historical reference. It is based on the English Voyager FAQ 1.7 (31 March 1998) by Benjamin C. Steeves, with mailing-list archives from Alan Crandall.

That FAQ described Voyager 2.95 / VoyagerNG, not V3. Many answers still apply to Voyager 3.5. Each topic below keeps the 1998 question, a short restatement of the original answer, and a 3.5 note.
